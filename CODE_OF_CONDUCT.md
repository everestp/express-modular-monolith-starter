# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate
- Focusing on technical and project goals
- Accepting differing viewpoints and experiences

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by contacting the maintainers.
